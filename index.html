Privacy Policy — Fruit Pop: Puzzle Games 5 in 1

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Fruit Pop: Puzzle Games 5 in 1 ("the game") is published by ABV Corporation. This page explains what the game does with your data.

Data we collect

None. The game does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no in-app purchases, and it does not connect to the internet.

Data kept on your device

Your progress (levels reached, best scores, sound and language settings) is saved in a file on your device only. It never leaves the device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Permissions

The game asks for no special permissions.

Children

The game contains no content that needs an age limit and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version starts using any data (for example, if advertising is added), this page will be updated before that version is released.
