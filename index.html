Privacy Policy — Fruit Pop: Puzzle Games 5 in 1

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Fruit Pop: Puzzle Games 5 in 1 ("the game") is published by ABV Corporation. This page explains what the game does with your data.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data. The game has no accounts of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no in-app purchases. The puzzles play without the internet; the only online feature is the optional Google Play Games sign-in described below.

Data kept on your device

Your progress (levels reached, best scores, sound and language settings) is saved in a file on your device only. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Google Play Games

The game can sign you in to Google Play Games Services, automatically if you are already signed in to it on your device. When you are signed in, your achievements and leaderboard scores are sent to Google, which processes them under Google's Privacy Policy, together with your Play Games profile (player name, avatar and player ID) that Google shows on leaderboards. We do not receive your e-mail address or other personal data. If you do not want this, sign out of Google Play Games in its settings; the game works fully without it.

Permissions

The game uses internet access only for Google Play Games and asks for no other special permissions.

Children

The game contains no content that needs an age limit, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version starts using any other data (for example, if advertising is added), this page will be updated before that version is released.
